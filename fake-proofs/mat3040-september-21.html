MAT3040 Advanced Linear Algebra · September 21

Statement

Let T:V→VT:V\to VT:V→V be a linear transformation with dim⁡V<∞\dim V<\inftydimV<∞. Then V=ker⁡T⊕im⁡TV=\ker T\oplus\operatorname{im}TV=kerT⊕imT.

Proposed proof

Since both the domain and codomain are VVV, we have ker⁡(T)≤Vandim⁡(T)≤V. \ker(T)\leq V \qquad\text{and}\qquad \operatorname{im}(T)\leq V. ker(T)≤Vandim(T)≤V.

In the proof of the Rank--Nullity Theorem, we have ker⁡(T)⊕U=V, \ker(T)\oplus U=V, ker(T)⊕U=V, where U≅T(U)=im⁡(T). U\cong T(U)=\operatorname{im}(T). U≅T(U)=im(T). Therefore one has ker⁡(T)⊕im⁡(T)=V. \ker(T)\oplus\operatorname{im}(T)=V. ker(T)⊕im(T)=V.
